# Lost in 100 Days of Zombies

Lost in 100 Days of Zombies is a Minecraft mod that transforms your survival experience into a thrilling zombie apocalypse. This mod introduces a variety of new zombie and mutant entities, each with unique 3D models and animations. It features custom weapons with sound effects, new armor sets, and a comprehensive survival system including stamina, power, and bleed-out mechanics. The mod replaces vanilla monsters with its own unique creatures and generates new structures like cities, military bases, and villages. Players must drink to stay hydrated and can be revived after death or experience a 120-second bleed-out effect.

## Install

1. Download the mod from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/yourusername/zombie-mutant-survival/releases).
2. Place the downloaded `.jar` file into the `mods` folder of your Minecraft installation directory.
3. Launch Minecraft with the Fabric loader installed.

## Use

1. Launch Minecraft and select the world where you want to play with the mod.
2. Explore the new structures and encounter the new zombie and mutant entities.
3. Use the custom weapons and armor to survive the apocalypse.
4. Manage your stamina and hydration levels to stay alive.
5. If you die, you can be revived or experience a bleed-out effect.
